Rank Intel

Rank Intel is a private analytics tool used by the owners of the RankSpike and RankSplash YouTube channels.

It reads each channel's YouTube Analytics (read-only) and public competitor information, and shows them in a private dashboard to help the channel owners plan better videos. It is not offered to the public and does not post, upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube.

Privacy policy

Rank Intel · Last updated November 7, 2026

Who this applies to

Rank Intel is a private tool used only by the owners of the RankSpike and RankSplash YouTube channels. It is not a public service.

What data Rank Intel accesses

  • With your permission through Google sign-in, read-only YouTube Analytics and YouTube channel data for the channels you choose to connect: for example views, watch time, audience retention, subscribers, traffic sources and audience geography.
  • Publicly available information about other YouTube channels and videos, such as titles, view counts and upload dates.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show analytics and reports in a private dashboard for the channel owners. Rank Intel never posts, uploads, edits or deletes anything on YouTube.

Sharing

Rank Intel does not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

Data is stored only as long as needed to produce the dashboard and reports. You can ask for it to be deleted at any time by emailing the contact below.

Google API Services

Rank Intel's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Rank Intel uses YouTube API Services and follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can remove Rank Intel's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
